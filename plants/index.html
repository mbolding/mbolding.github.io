The page makes a safety claim, so it should say where it comes from: all six are listed as non-toxic to cats in the ASPCA's toxic and non-toxic plant database.

Non-toxic is not the same as harmless. A cat that eats enough of anything will be sick, and nurseries do sometimes mislabel. Check the botanical name.
