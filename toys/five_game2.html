Breakthrough Five

Two players, one board, five squares a side. Get any piece to the opposite back rank and you win.

Blue to move.

How it moves

  • Pawn (🔵🔴) — one square in any direction.
  • Rook (🏰🏯) — any distance along a rank or file, not through pieces.
  • Bishop (🔷🔶) — any distance diagonally, not through pieces.
  • Landing on an opposing piece captures it.
  • Tap or click a piece to select it, then tap a highlighted square. Arrow keys and Enter work too.
