Frontier Expedition

Navigate the map, manage your stores, and reach the blue square in the top right corner. Light blue squares are supply caches.

Costs

  • Every move costs 1 food.
  • Resting costs 1 food and restores 1 health.
  • Hunting costs 1 supply and brings back a variable amount of food.
  • Exploring costs 1 food, and a square only pays out once.

Controls

  • Move: arrow keys, WASD, or H J K L, or the on-screen pad
  • Rest: R
  • Hunt: F
  • Explore: E
  • On a touch screen, swipe across the map to move

Ending

Run out of food, supplies or health and the expedition fails. Reach the pass and it succeeds, scored by what you found on the way.
