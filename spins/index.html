Spins

Animated, interactive figures for explaining magnetic resonance at the board: Larmor precession, coherence and its loss, the spin echo, chemical shift, and the magnetism of the materials we carry into the scanner room. Every figure runs offline, adapts to the projector, and states its own teaching point.
