Affinity Mapping Guide & Controls

      
      

        

          
Pointer & Touch

          
    
            
  • Drag & Drop: Grab any card and drag it into a cluster or back into the tray. Works with mouse, trackpad, and touchscreen.
    • 
            
  • Click-to-Move: Click any card to select it (glowing highlight), then click the target cluster or tray to place it immediately. Ideal for wireless clickers or when students call out moves from the room.
    • 
          

        


        

          
Keyboard Shortcuts

          
    
            
  • Tab / ← → ↑ ↓ : Navigate through cards
    • 
            
  • Enter / Space : Pick up / select focused card
    • 
            
  • 1 through 9 : Drop selected card into cluster 1 through 9
    • 
            
  • 0 : Drop selected card back into the tray
    • 
            
  • Esc : Cancel card selection / close popups
    • 
          

        


        

          
Classroom Features

          
    
            
  • Section Colors: Off by default. Turn on during discussion to reveal undergrad (425) vs grad (625) origins.
    • 
            
  • Big Text: Scales up fonts and cards for back-of-the-room projector visibility.
    • 
            
  • Reveal Themes: Open the instructor comparison panel. Click any theme to highlight where its ideas landed across student clusters.
    • 
            
  • Autosave: All arrangements persist automatically in your browser's local storage.
