Sorting millions of LORs by angle \theta and radial offset s produces the Sinogram via the Radon transform. Mathematical Foundations of PET Tomography

1. The Radon Transform & Sinogram Geometry:

In 2D PET, each line integral across tracer activity f(x, y) is parameterized by normal angle \theta \in [0, \pi] and signed radial displacement s from the scanner isocenter: p(s, \theta) = \int \int f(x, y) \delta(x \cos\theta + y \sin\theta - s) dx dy. A single isolated hot tumor at polar coordinate (r_0, \phi_0) traces a pure sine wave across the Radon plane: s(\theta) = r_0 \sin(\theta - \phi_0). The sinogram is the collection of all such intersecting sinusoids.

2. Unfiltered vs. Filtered Backprojection:

Projecting sinogram values back along each LOR without filtering accumulates the image, but introduces an intrinsic 1/r point-spread blurring kernel (f_{\text{unfiltered}} = f \ast \frac{1}{r}). In Fourier space, the Fourier Slice Theorem proves that 1D projections correspond to radial slices through 2D k-space. The Ram-Lak ramp filter (|k|) compensates exactly for the high density of radial sampling near the origin. Apodizing with a Hann window (H(k) = 0.5 + 0.5\cos(\pi k / k_{\text{max}})) prevents high-frequency Poisson noise blow-up.

3. Iterative MLEM Reconstruction:

Filtered Backprojection assumes deterministic, continuous line integrals and easily produces negative artifactual pixel values in low-count regions. Maximum Likelihood Expectation Maximization (MLEM) models the discrete Poisson statistics of radioactive decay: \hat{f}_j^{(n+1)} = \hat{f}_j^{(n)} \frac{1}{\sum_i c_{ij}} \sum_i c_{ij} \frac{p_i}{\sum_k c_{ik} \hat{f}_k^{(n)}}. Each iteration compares projected estimates against actual measured counts, guaranteeing strictly non-negative activity and superior signal-to-noise ratios.

4. 511 keV Photon Attenuation Correction:

Before escaping the patient's body, 511 keV photons can undergo Compton scattering or photoelectric absorption. The survival probability along an LOR is A_i = \exp(-\int \mu(x,y) dl), independent of where along the LOR the emission took place! Without attenuation correction (CT or MRI-based \mu-map), deep brain structures appear artificially hypometabolic while the cortical periphery creates false hypermetabolic rims.
