Interactive Neuroimaging Demos

Methods in Human Neuroimaging — NBL 425/625

A suite of interactive web-based simulations designed for NBL 425/625 to explore the core physical principles, spatial encoding schemes, contrast mechanisms, statistical modeling, and artifact origins in human neuroimaging.

Course Demos

  • EPI Geometric Distortion & Susceptibility Dropout

    EPI Artifacts · Field Offsets ΔB0, Bandwidth & TOPUP Unwarping

    Visualizes how air-tissue interfaces (paranasal sinuses) interact with the low phase-encoding bandwidth of Echo Planar Imaging to cause geometric stretching (P→A) vs. compression (A→P) and intra-voxel signal blackout, and how reversed phase-encode calibration (TOPUP) restores geometry.

  • fMRI GLM & Convolution Studio

    fMRI Analysis · HRF Convolution, Design Matrices, Prewhitening & OLS Regression

    Design cognitive timelines (block vs. event-related), convolve impulses with the canonical double-gamma Hemodynamic Response Function, inspect collinearity and variance inflation (VIF), and solve the General Linear Model ($Y = X\beta + \epsilon$) in real time with statistical contrasts ($t$-tests). Colour the noise with physiological oscillations and AR(1) autocorrelation, switch on prewhitening, and audit the null false-positive rate to see how far ordinary least squares over-states its own significance when the residuals are not independent.

  • fMRI Preprocessing Pipeline Workbench

    fMRI Analysis · Realignment, Slice Timing, Smoothing & High-Pass Filtering

    Acquire a synthetic run corrupted by head motion, interleaved slice timing, drift and spatially correlated physiological noise, then switch each preprocessing stage on and off and watch the statistics respond. Realignment parameters are genuinely estimated by image registration rather than read back from the simulation, so the residual error is real. Two activation foci of different size expose the matched-filter trade in spatial smoothing, including the point where a larger kernel fuses them into one.

  • Whole-Brain Thresholding & Multiple Comparisons

    Statistical Inference · Uncorrected, Bonferroni, FDR & Cluster Extent

    Run a real voxelwise GLM across a whole slice and threshold the resulting $t$-map four ways side by side. The cluster-extent threshold is calibrated by Monte Carlo against null data rather than assumed, and a built-in audit measures each method's family-wise error rate by analysing 200 datasets containing nothing at all. Includes the Benjamini–Hochberg ladder plot and the dead-salmon demonstration.

  • Group-Level Inference: Fixed vs. Random Effects

    Second-Level Analysis · Variance Components, Power & Generalisation

    Simulate a full group study, run both second-level analyses on identical data, and see why fixed effects cannot support a claim about people. A forest plot contrasts the two confidence intervals, Monte Carlo power curves show when scanning longer stops helping and recruiting is the only lever left, and a validity audit measures how often each analysis invents a population effect that is not there.

  • Free Induction Decay (FID) & Spin Relaxation

    Induction Physics · Faraday's Law, Mxy Evolution, T1 Recovery & T2* Dephasing

    Directly connects the 3D time evolution of the net magnetic moment to the voltage signal induced in an RF receiver coil. Remembers longitudinal $M_z$ memory across pulses to demonstrate $T_1$ saturation, inversion recovery, and the null point.

  • Gradient Encoding Visualizer

    Spatial Encoding · Frequency, Phase & Slice Selection

    Visualizes how linear magnetic field gradients make the Larmor precession frequency position-dependent, demonstrating slice selection (Gz), phase encoding blips (Gy), and frequency encoding readout (Gx).

  • Pulse Sequence Timing Diagram

    Sequence Engineering · Spin Echo, TR & TE

    An interactive spin-echo timing diagram. Adjust TE to see the 180° refocusing pulse and echo move synchronously; adjust TR to explore repetition timing, dead time, and phase-encode step evolution.

  • TR/TE Contrast Explorer

    Contrast Weighting · T1, T2 & Proton Density

    Manipulate TR and TE sliders to see real-time signal calculations across four tissue types (White Matter, Gray Matter, CSF, and Fat), dynamically identifying T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and PD-weighted regimes.

  • k-space Explorer

    Fourier Reconstruction · Central vs. Peripheral Spatial Frequencies

    Filter k-space with a variable centered window to observe how the center carries coarse image contrast while the periphery contributes edge sharpness. Illustrates Gibbs truncation ringing artifacts.

  • MRI Artifact Simulator

    Artifact Physics · Chemical Shift, Aliasing & Motion

    Introduce deliberate k-space and image distortions to inspect Chemical Shift (fat-water frequency displacement), Aliasing / Wrap-around (reduced FOV), and Motion Ghosting (phase-encode modulation) with variable severity.

  • DTI Tensor & Tractography Explorer

    Diffusion · Eigenvalues, FA, Streamline Integration & Crossing Fibers

    Build a diffusion tensor from its eigenvalues and orientation, read off FA, MD and the Westin linearity / planarity / sphericity measures, and see the resulting signal attenuation across a 6-, 15- or 30-direction gradient scheme. A synthetic crossing then shows why deterministic tractography stops at fiber intersections, and why loosening the turning-angle limit to get through produces anatomically false tracts.

  • fNIRS Modified Beer–Lambert Unmixer

    Optical Imaging · Extinction Spectra, Isosbestic Point & Matrix Conditioning

    Choose two laser wavelengths inside the near-infrared window and see how that single decision determines whether oxy- and deoxy-haemoglobin can be separated at all. Includes the measured extinction spectra, the Modified Beer-Lambert forward model, and a simulated block-design recording solved back to concentrations, with the condition number of the 2x2 system reported live.

  • PET Pharmacokinetics & 2-Tissue Compartment Simulator

    Molecular Imaging · Rate Constants, TACs & Patlak/Logan Plots

    Model intravenous radiotracer delivery across the blood-brain barrier into free and metabolically trapped or receptor-bound compartments. Evaluate real-time Time-Activity Curves and perform Patlak (irreversible FDG glucose) and Logan (reversible ligand) graphical linearizations.

  • EEG Forward Problem & Scalp Potential Mapper

    Electrophysiology · 3-Shell Head Model, Volume Conduction & 10-20 Topomaps

    Position and orient equivalent current dipoles in a 3-shell concentric head model (Brain, high-resistivity Skull, Scalp). Observe how volume conduction through the resistive skull blurs focal cortical activity, and inspect the resulting 10-20 scalp electrode voltage distributions.

  • EEG vs. MEG: Radial vs. Tangential Dipole Sensitivity

    Electromagnetism · Biot-Savart Law, Gyral vs. Sulcal Sources & Sensor Physics

    Explore why MEG is completely blind to radial dipoles on gyral crowns and selectively records tangential dipoles in sulcal banks, whereas EEG detects both. Includes dual side-by-side electric potential and magnetic flux density topomaps.

  • Simultaneous EEG-fMRI Artifact Decontamination Workbench

    Multi-Modal Processing · Gradient Spikes, BCG Removal & ERP Recovery

    Step through an interactive multi-stage decontamination pipeline to strip away 20 millivolt imaging gradient switching bursts via Average Artifact Subtraction (AAS) and cardiac ballistocardiogram (BCG) artifacts via Optimal Basis Sets (OBS), unmasking the clean microvolt visual P100 evoked response.

  • Structural Connectome Matrix & Graph Theory Studio

    Connectomics · Adjacency Matrix, Small-Worldness, Hubs & Targeted Attack Resilience

    Construct whole-brain structural connectivity matrices from streamline tractography. Calculate characteristic path length, clustering coefficient, small-worldness (σ), and test network attack vulnerability by comparing random axonal damage against targeted rich-club hub lesions.

  • PET Sinogram & Tomographic Reconstruction Workbench

    Tomography · Coincidence LORs, Radon Sinograms, FBP & Iterative MLEM

    Map positron annihilation coincidence lines of response into Radon sinograms. Invert the intrinsic 1/r backprojection blurring with Ram-Lak ramp and Hann filters, and run iterative Poisson MLEM with 511 keV tissue attenuation correction.

  • EEG Inverse Problem & Source Localization Studio

    Source Localization · Underdetermined Inverses, MNE, Depth Weighting & LCMV Beamformers

    Reconstruct cortical current dipoles from 16-channel scalp electric potentials. Compare unregularized pseudoinverse noise explosion against Tikhonov Minimum Norm Estimation (MNE), depth-weighted wMNE, and spatial LCMV beamforming.

  • fNIRS Photon Transport & Short-Channel Scalp Regression

    Diffuse Optics · Monte Carlo Transport, Banana Paths & Scalp Mayer Wave Decontamination

    Simulate photon packet scattering random walks through layered scalp, skull, and brain cortex. Test dual-distance optodes to see how short-channel (8 mm) regression strips away confounding systemic blood pressure Mayer waves (0.1 Hz) to isolate true cortical activation.

  • MRS Metabolite Basis Set & LCModel Spectral Fitter

    Spectroscopy · Chemical Shift, Metabolite Basis Vectors, Splines & CRLB Quantitation

    Deconstruct in-vivo 1H MR spectra into basis spectra for NAA, Creatine, Choline, Myo-inositol, Glutamate, and Lactate. Explore B0 field dispersion (1.5T vs 3T vs 7T), shim linewidths, macromolecular baselines, and Cramer-Rao Lower Bounds (CRLB %SD) across clinical tumor and stroke presets.

  • Multi-Modal Fusion & Representational Similarity Analysis (RSA)

    Data Fusion · Second-Order Isomorphism, Millisecond Dynamics & Spatial Categorization

    Bridge millisecond EEG dynamics and millimeter fMRI spatial activations without forced source constraints. Track condition-by-condition Representational Dissimilarity Matrices (RDMs) across post-stimulus time to map the spatiotemporal hierarchy from V1 to LOC and FFA.

  • Optically Pumped Magnetometers (OPM) & Wearable MEG Studio

    Emerging Quantum Modalities · SERF Physics, Standoff Distance, Pediatric Imaging & Motion Robustness

    Compare room-temperature wearable OPM arrays against rigid cryogenic SQUID helmets. Test how eliminating the 25-35 mm vacuum dewar gap yields a 4-5x boost in magnetic flux (B ∝ 1/r^2), enables pediatric imaging, and allows natural head motion with active background nulling coils.

  • Brain-Wide Association Studies (BWAS) Power & Replication Simulator

    Big Data · Statistical Power, Winner's Curse Effect Inflation & Out-of-Sample Replication

    Simulate brain-behavior association studies across sample sizes from N = 25 to N = 4,000. Discover why modest true effects (|r| ≈ 0.10) produce severe effect size inflation (r_obs ≈ 0.50) in underpowered cohorts, and run 1,000-study Monte Carlo audits to see why thousands of scans are required for honest replication.
