Worksheet 2.1: Neuropathological Hallmarks & Biomarker Mapping

Translate clinical neuropathology into measurable biophysical contrast mechanisms. Deconstruct the biological cascade of your team's chosen disorder, delineate anatomical localization and physical scale, and evaluate viable non-invasive sensing modalities.

● Draft synced locally
Engineering Design Context: A diagnostic neurotechnology device succeeds or fails based on whether its biophysical transduction mechanism couples directly to the primary pathological hallmark of the disease. In this worksheet:
  • Isolate 2–4 verified cellular or physiological hallmarks (e.g., microglial activation, tau paired helical filaments, BBB disruption, cortical microdysplasia, paroxysmal depolarizing shifts).
  • Specify the exact spatial locus, tissue depth, and physical dimension of each lesion or event.
  • Connect each biological alteration to a physical property change (e.g., magnetic susceptibility \(\chi\), water diffusion coefficient \(D\), optical absorption \(\mu_a\), dielectric permittivity \(\varepsilon\), dipole current \(Q\)).
