Interactive Rubric Scorecards

Select a milestone below to view detailed criteria or calculate an evaluation score in real time.

01. Clinical Need & Stakeholder Alignment 0 / 25 pts

Clarity of target disorder definition, unmet diagnostic gap, stakeholder interview rigor (neurologists, neuroradiologists), and clinical sensitivity/specificity targets.

Exemplary
23–25 pts
Target pathology precisely bounded; quantified clinical need supported by primary stakeholder interviews; unambiguous diagnostic performance envelope.
Proficient
19–22 pts
Clear clinical condition and literature rationale; stakeholder needs identified but missing granular quantitative bounds.
Developing
15–18 pts
Vague disease framing; generic stakeholder requirements lacking clinical validation or realistic workflow integration.
Unsatisfactory
0–14 pts
Superficial pathology overview; no evidence of stakeholder alignment or target diagnostic criteria.
02. Biophysical Contrast & Transduction Rigor 0 / 25 pts

Mathematical/physical modeling of the neural contrast mechanism (dipole, BOLD, optical absorption, acoustic impedance), signal propagation, SNR budget, and spatiotemporal resolution limits.

Exemplary
23–25 pts
Rigorous forward physics formulation; accurate SNR vs. tissue depth modeling; clear articulation of physical vs. biological noise sources.
Proficient
19–22 pts
Correct physical principles; sound order-of-magnitude estimates; minor gaps in skull attenuation or noise modeling.
Developing
15–18 pts
Qualitative descriptions only; lacks mathematical modeling of signal decay, SNR, or resolution trade-offs.
Unsatisfactory
0–14 pts
Flawed biophysical assumptions or physically unfeasible contrast mechanisms proposed.
03. Preliminary Architecture & Feasibility 0 / 25 pts

System block diagram, sensor placement geometry, channel count justification, preliminary component selection, and initial safety envelope.

Exemplary
23–25 pts
Comprehensive system topology; fully justified channel array geometry; clear signal chain from transducer to ADC with component bounds.
Proficient
19–22 pts
Solid block diagram; reasonable sensor array layout; minor omissions in interface electronics or data bandwidth.
Developing
15–18 pts
High-level black-box diagrams; vague sensor specs; insufficient consideration of hardware feasibility.
Unsatisfactory
0–14 pts
Missing architecture; disconnected subsystems; no hardware rationale.
04. Defense, Visual Concept Pin-Up & Q&A 0 / 25 pts

12-minute oral presentation delivery, concept pin-up poster visual clarity, poise during faculty grilling, and equitable interdisciplinary team speaking.

Exemplary
23–25 pts
Flawless time management; publication-quality schematics; incisive and confident responses to rigorous panel critique; seamless team parity.
Proficient
19–22 pts
Clear delivery; good visuals; solid answers to most questions with minor hesitation on deep edge cases.
Developing
15–18 pts
Rushed or over time; cluttered pin-up boards; defensive or uncertain answers during Q&A; unbalanced participation.
Unsatisfactory
0–14 pts
Disorganized presentation; illegible visuals; inability to address panel questions.
01. End-to-End Systems Engineering & Signal Chain 0 / 25 pts

Full circuit and instrumentation architecture, analog front-end (AFE), filtering, sampling rates, dynamic range, data throughput, and mechanical form factor.

02. Inverse Problem & Image Reconstruction Rigor 0 / 25 pts

Mathematical inverse formulation, regularization choices (L1/L2, beamforming, dipole fits), motion artifact rejection algorithms, and quantitative reconstruction SNR.

03. Safety, SAR / Thermal & FDA Regulatory Strategy 0 / 25 pts

Compliance with IEEE/IEC safety standards, Specific Absorption Rate (SAR), acoustic limits, electrical isolation, cybersecurity, and credible FDA 510(k) predicate / De Novo roadmap.

04. Verification Plan & IRB Clinical Protocol Draft 0 / 25 pts

Bench/phantom verification protocol, sample size calculation, statistical endpoints, risk-benefit analysis, and complete draft of IRB human subjects protocol.

01. Clinical Value Proposition & Pitch Narrative 0 / 25 pts

Compelling framing of the grand challenge, healthcare economic impact, patient-centric workflow, and persuasive translation thesis.

02. Live Software / Simulation Demonstration 0 / 25 pts

Interactive execution of virtual prototype simulation, real-time noise injection, inverse reconstruction visualization, and codebase reproducibility.

03. Defense Under Expert Panel Grilling 0 / 25 pts

Mastery of technical edge cases, honest acknowledgment of limitations, rigorous justification of assumptions, and intellectual agility during Q&A.

04. Visual Polish, Deck Quality & Team Synergies 0 / 25 pts

Executive-level visual design, synchronized multimedia, seamless cross-disciplinary speaking transitions, and exemplary professional presence.
