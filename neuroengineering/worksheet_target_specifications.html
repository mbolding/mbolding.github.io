Worksheet 2.3: Diagnostic Target Specifications & Performance Envelope

Translate qualitative stakeholder needs into quantitative engineering specifications. Establish strict bounds for diagnostic accuracy (sensitivity, specificity, ROC AUC, PPV/NPV given disease prevalence), spatiotemporal resolution, and clinical usability constraints.

● Draft synced locally
Target Product Profile (TPP) Rigor: In medical device engineering, vague goals like "high resolution" or "good accuracy" result in project failure. Specifications must be:
  • Quantitative: State exact physical numbers with standard units (e.g. \(1.2 \text{ mm isotropic}\), \(250 \text{ Hz}\), \(>92\% \text{ sensitivity}\)).
  • Bounded by Trade-offs: High spatial resolution degrades temporal resolution and SNR (\(\text{SNR} \propto \Delta x \Delta y \Delta z \sqrt{T_{\text{acq}}}\)).
  • Contextualized by Bayesian Prevalence: In a screening population where disease prevalence is 2%, a 90% specific test generates 5 times more false alarms than true positives!
