Worksheet 7.1: Preliminary Design Review (PDR) & Evaluation Rubric

Formal design milestone assessment for student neurotechnologies. Evaluates biophysical contrast justification, sensor hardware architecture, noise budget margin, signal encoding/reconstruction strategy, and Grand Challenge 06 (Neonatal & Pediatric Brain Monitoring) alignment.

01.

Project Identification & Review Mode

👶 Grand Challenge 06 Core Criteria Projects focused on neonatal and pediatric neuromonitoring must explicitly defend mechanical compliance (skin pressure < 20 mmHg, no abrasion), ambient NICU rejection (phototherapy lighting, incubator radiant heat), and biomarker clinical validity (e.g., FTOE, pulsatility index, burst suppression).
